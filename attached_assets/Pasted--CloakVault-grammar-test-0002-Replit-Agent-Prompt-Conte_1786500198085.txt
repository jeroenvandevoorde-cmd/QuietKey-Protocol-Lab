# CloakVault grammar-test-0002: Replit Agent Prompt

## Context (read this first)

You are building the successor to grammar-test-0001 in the existing CloakVault reference implementation. 0001 carries the codeword correctly (242 slots, 1044-bit capacity over the 968-bit RS(121,93) codeword) but failed the human plausibility gate: number-agreement errors, duplicate ingredient lines, titles naming ingredients absent from the recipe, and instructions that cook ingredients not present in the ingredient list. The deeper cause is structural: slot words are selected by ciphertext bits, which are effectively random, and 0001 draws every slot independently from an unconditioned lexicon. Random independent draws produce culinary nonsense.

Goal of 0002: every generated document reads as a real recipe and is broadly cookable, at maximum information density, measured against a one-A4-page target. Broadly cookable means a competent home cook following it produces an edible result and no step is physically nonsensical. The automated suite proves the invariants; final cookability is judged by a human read of 20 exported documents. Your job is to get the build to that gate with honest numbers.

Everything stays deterministic. Encode and decode are pure functions of grammar plus codeword. No LLM anywhere, no randomness beyond the ciphertext bits.

## Scope and non-negotiable rules

- Do not touch: crypto core, capsule format, RS(121,93), parity budget, protocol test vectors. The codeword is 968 bits, fixed.
- Grammars are versioned, immutable artifacts. grammar-test-0001 stays in the repo byte-identical and decodable, and all its existing tests keep passing. All new work lands as grammar-test-0002 alongside it.
- Decoder architecture rules are unchanged: canonicalize, align on fixed anchor prose, slot match is exact or edit distance exactly 1 to a unique candidate in that slot's candidate set, otherwise an erasure at the known position; junk tokens between expected tokens are skippable. Never infer a word from semantic context.
- Conditioning rule, structural and absolute: a slot's candidate set may depend on the grammar version and the archetype, never on another slot's decoded value, with one exception defined in Task 2 for derived references into the chosen ingredient set. One misread slot must never change the candidate set of another slot.
- Client-side only, no storage APIs, no network requests, never log seeds or keys, the TEST USE ONLY banner stays.

## Task 1: Archetype layer

Define 4 to 8 dish archetypes (for example: traybake, stew, grain salad, soup, skillet saute). The leading bits of the codeword select the archetype. The archetype selects the entire fixed template: title pattern, section structure, connective prose, step skeletons, and every conditional lexicon.

The archetype is recovered structurally, not from a fragile slot: each archetype's fixed anchor prose is distinct, so template identification during alignment recovers the archetype bits with the whole document voting. If alignment cannot establish the archetype unambiguously, decode fails with an explicit error. Never guess.

## Task 2: Mechanical invariants, all deterministic

- Grammatical number agreement everywhere: quantity, unit, and noun always agree.
- No duplicate ingredients: encode the ingredient list as an ordered selection without replacement (rank and unrank over permutations), with exact bit accounting in the capacity report.
- Titles are derived from the chosen key ingredients and carry zero or few bits. A title may only name ingredients present in the document.
- Steps reference only chosen ingredients. Step-level ingredient mentions are derived references into the already-chosen ingredient set: either fully derived at zero bits or drawing log2 of the usable subset, your choice per slot, documented in the capacity report. This is the one permitted dependence on other slots, and it must degrade safely: if the referenced ingredient slot is an erasure, the step mention becomes an erasure too, never a wrong-set match.

## Task 3: Culinary conditioning

Quantity lexicons conditioned on ingredient class: spices in pinches and teaspoons, produce in grams and handfuls, nothing at the scale of twelve pounds in a serves-four dish. Verb and time lexicons conditioned on method and ingredient class: no whisked buckwheat, no seventy-five-minute sears. Temperatures and times land in plausible ranges for the method. The target is that any codeword produces a coherent dish of its archetype.

Lexicon hygiene: within any one candidate set, entries must be pairwise Levenshtein distance 2 or more (hard build failure below 2), distance 3 or more where feasible, with a distance-distribution report per lexicon.

## Task 4: Density, measured not forced

- Typography constants, fixed for measurement and not to be changed: A4, single-sided, single column, 11pt serif, 20mm margins. The renderer computes projected page count from these.
- Ingredient section renders as run-in prose per group ("For the spice mix: a level pinch of cinnamon, a generous pinch of sumac, ...") rather than one line per item, where the archetype allows.
- Trim connective prose for density without degrading naturalness.
- Total capacity must be at least 968 bits with at most 5 percent headroom above it; delete unused capacity beyond that.

## Task 5: Instrumentation

Build-time report for the grammar: total capacity bits, bits by section, average bits per slot, bits per rendered word, rendered word count, projected page count at the fixed typography, per-lexicon distance distributions, and the without-replacement bit accounting. The live inspector panel shows the same headline numbers as today, now for 0002.

## Task 6: Grammar registration and auto-detect

Register both grammars. Recover auto-detects the grammar by anchor alignment score across registered grammars: unambiguous winner proceeds, ambiguity or no match fails with an explicit error naming the candidates. 0001 documents must still decode as 0001.

## Task 7: The gate packet

Export 20 grammar-test-0002 documents (deterministic: fixed test mnemonic, 20 fixed distinct Vault Keys) as PDFs or print-ready pages at the fixed typography, plus the build report, as the human-gate packet.

Stop at the gate. If one page is not reachable at the achieved plausibility level, report the exact gap: rendered words over budget, bits per rendered word achieved versus the roughly 1.5 needed for one page. Do not shrink type, do not cut margins, do not degrade plausibility, do not touch payload or parity to force the fit. That trade is a human decision, not yours.

## Acceptance criteria

1. grammar-test-0001 byte-identical, all its tests green.
2. 0002 round trip: encode, render, canonicalize, decode is the identity with zero erasures on clean text, across at least 50 distinct codewords.
3. Property tests across at least 500 random codewords: no duplicate ingredients, full number agreement, titles and steps reference only present ingredients, all quantities and times within their class ranges.
4. The seeded 100-case damage-model fuzz suite runs green against 0002 documents.
5. Auto-detect: 0001 docs decode as 0001, 0002 as 0002, ambiguity produces the explicit error.
6. Build report generated with every metric in Task 5, including projected page count.
7. The 20-document gate packet and the report are exported and linked from the self-test page.
8. Crypto and RS suites untouched and green. Storage-API and network greps still zero.

## What not to do

No LLM, no randomness beyond ciphertext bits, no protocol or parity changes, no typography below the fixed constants, no editing 0001, no slot-on-slot conditioning beyond the Task 2 derived references, no new UI beyond the inspector numbers and the auto-detect error, no storage, no network. If the page does not fit, you report the gap and stop: you do not decide the trade.
