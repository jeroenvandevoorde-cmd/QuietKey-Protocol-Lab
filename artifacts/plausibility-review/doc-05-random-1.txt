# vibrant marinated cauliflower
with jicama and chervil

Serves six | Prep thirty minutes | Cook twenty-five minutes

## Ingredients
- five sprigs fennel, minced
- two handfuls celery, pickled
- eight grams plantains, quartered
- one bunches peppers, halved
- five teaspoons fennel, shredded
- six teaspoons celeriac, diced
- one sprigs oats, pickled
- twelve sprigs corn, quartered
- twelve tablespoons asparagus and taro, julienned
- two handfuls orzo and radishes, trimmed
- five strips lentils and asparagus, shredded
- six strips rapini and mache, grated
- one cloves broccoli and broccoli, julienned
- eight strips jicama and tomatoes, quartered
- eight tablespoons kale and spinach, blanched
- six pinches peppers and escarole, quartered
- eight slices fresh lavender
- one pinches fresh savory
- four slices fresh dill
- five bunches fresh sage
- six cups fresh sage
- twelve heads fresh lavender
- a level pinch of coriander
- a whole pinch of paprika
- a whole pinch of cumin
- a large pinch of mace
- a whole pinch of ginger
- a level pinch of cayenne

## Instructions
1. broil the leeks in a wok over low heat with onions and sumac for forty minutes until softened.
2. Add parsnips along with corn, steam quickly for twelve minutes, then squeeze with chives.
3. simmer the zucchini in a terrine over high heat with chard and turmeric for fifteen minutes until bubbling.
4. Add celery along with beets, roast gently for two minutes, then plate with lavender.
5. caramelize the carrots in a skillet over high heat with watercress and cayenne for eighteen minutes until browned.
6. Add oats along with watercress, caramelize gently for twelve minutes, then squeeze with dill.
7. heat the kohlrabi in a casserole over high heat with onions and nutmeg for seventy-five minutes until thickened.
8. Add garlic along with buckwheat, poach evenly for three minutes, then fold with mint.
9. whisk the radishes in a saucepan over medium-high heat with barley and ginger for fifteen minutes until golden.
10. Add celeriac along with teff, deglaze gently for four minutes, then rest with savory.
11. season the purslane in a wok over low heat with orzo and cayenne for twenty-five minutes until tender.
12. Add sunchokes along with amaranth, poach carefully for twelve minutes, then squeeze with dill.
13. caramelize the buckwheat in a roaster over gentle heat with romanesco and nutmeg for twelve minutes until glossy.
14. Add plantains along with kamut, heat carefully for five minutes, then spoon with rosemary.

## Notes
- even resting of endive deepens the overall flavor.
- careful reducing of lentils deepens the overall flavor.
- patient caramelizing of onions deepens the overall flavor.
- proper resting of shallots deepens the overall flavor.
- careful straining of radishes deepens the overall flavor.

## Variations
- vegetarian variation: substitute beets with eggplant.
- vegan variation: substitute barley with peppers.
- vegan variation: substitute oats with fennel.
- creamier variation: substitute chicory with tomatoes.
