# seasonal baked farro
with edamame and sorrel

Serves four | Prep forty minutes | Cook twenty minutes

## Ingredients
- one heads peppers, minced
- two ounces turnips, grated
- six cloves sorghum, halved
- six slices celeriac, sliced
- four heads orzo, halved
- six slices celery, sliced
- eight tablespoons barley, pickled
- twelve cups rye, halved
- eight wedges radicchio and cabbage, halved
- twelve slices kamut and rapini, blanched
- five ounces garlic and fennel, peeled
- two pounds peas and sunchokes, shredded
- three pounds garlic and potatoes, bruised
- six bunches cabbage and zucchini, diced
- five slices chickpeas and spinach, bruised
- five slices radishes and onions, minced
- five handfuls fresh dill
- twelve knobs fresh lavender
- two teaspoons fresh chervil
- four pounds fresh oregano
- three knobs fresh marjoram
- eight teaspoons fresh dill
- a heaping pinch of pepper
- a light pinch of paprika
- a heaping pinch of coriander
- a scant pinch of pepper
- a small pinch of saffron
- a light pinch of mace

## Instructions
1. sear the rapini in a griddle over low heat with asparagus and cinnamon for twelve minutes until firm.
2. Add buckwheat along with peppers, simmer vigorously for two minutes, then portion with rosemary.
3. bake the celery in a fryer over high heat with artichokes and sumac for forty-five minutes until silky.
4. Add watercress along with beets, broil steadily for two minutes, then plate with oregano.
5. caramelize the asparagus in a terrine over low heat with celery and ginger for twenty-five minutes until silky.
6. Add rye along with chickpeas, caramelize quickly for two minutes, then top with rosemary.
7. poach the cauliflower in a roaster over intense heat with taro and saffron for twelve minutes until reduced.
8. Add orzo along with chickpeas, sear vigorously for five minutes, then divide with sage.
9. deglaze the kamut in a fryer over moderate heat with sunchokes and pepper for sixty minutes until reduced.
10. Add tomatoes along with orzo, fry gently for five minutes, then squeeze with dill.
11. caramelize the barley in a casserole over medium-low heat with chickpeas and mace for seventy-five minutes until reduced.
12. Add carrots along with shallots, fry quickly for five minutes, then spoon with cilantro.
13. caramelize the kamut in a brazier over low heat with teff and nutmeg for forty-five minutes until browned.
14. Add zucchini along with purslane, reduce gently for four minutes, then arrange with cilantro.

## Notes
- gentle browning of edamame deepens the overall flavor.
- adequate soaking of broccoli deepens the overall flavor.
- patient marinating of sunchokes deepens the overall flavor.
- careful steaming of radishes deepens the overall flavor.
- proper sieving of celeriac deepens the overall flavor.

## Variations
- crunchier variation: substitute romanesco with peas.
- heartier variation: substitute frisee with asparagus.
- richer variation: substitute barley with sorghum.
- bolder variation: substitute artichokes with rapini.
