While the tray roasts: a shaved fennel salad

Something sharp and cold to sit beside all that sweetness.

Serves 4 as a side. Ten minutes.

1 large fennel bulb, shaved as thinly as you can
2 celery sticks, sliced on an angle
a small bunch of mint, leaves picked
50g rocket
juice of half a lemon
3 tablespoons olive oil
a pinch of sea salt

1. Toss the fennel, celery, mint and rocket together in a wide bowl.

2. Whisk the lemon juice with the oil and salt, pour it over just before serving, and turn the salad gently with your hands.

Variations on the traybake

For a milder tray, halve the harissa and stir a spoonful of yoghurt through the glaze. For extra crunch, add the almonds for the last ten minutes of roasting instead of at the end. In summer, swap the parsnips and potatoes for peppers and courgettes and cut the second roast to fifteen minutes.

If you are cooking for two, halve everything but keep the same tray; the vegetables brown better with room to breathe.
