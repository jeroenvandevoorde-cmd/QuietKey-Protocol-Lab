Three ways with the jar

Bircher bowl (serves 1)
50 g granola
100 g natural yoghurt
75 ml whole milk
1 small apple, coarsely grated

Stir everything together the night before and keep it covered in the fridge. In the morning loosen it with a splash more milk and finish with 1 tsp honey.

Warm pears (serves 2)
2 ripe pears, halved and cored
15 g butter
2 tbsp granola

Fry the pears cut-side down in the butter for 6 min over a medium heat until golden, then fill each hollow with granola and a spoonful of yoghurt.

Traybake topping
Scatter 75 g of granola over any fruit crumble for the last 10 min of baking instead of a crumble lid; it saves making a second mix and bakes to a deeper crunch.

Keeping and swaps
The base recipe takes any dried fruit at the same weights; figs or cranberries are the best of the rest. For a nut-free jar, replace the nuts with the same weight of extra seeds and bake for 5 min less. The jar keeps 3 weeks sealed, or 2 months in the freezer.
