ROASTED TOMATO AND WHITE BEAN STEW
A simple supper for four

INGREDIENTS
2 tablespoons olive oil
1 medium yellow onion, finely chopped
2 garlic cloves, minced
1 teaspoon smoked paprika
1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
2 cans (400 g each) white beans, drained and rinsed
1 can (400 g) chopped tomatoes
250 ml vegetable stock
1 tablespoon tomato paste
1 teaspoon red wine vinegar
Salt and black pepper
A small handful of flat-leaf parsley

METHOD
1. Warm the olive oil in a wide saucepan over medium heat.
2. Add the onion and a small pinch of salt. Cook for 8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until soft.
3. Add the garlic, paprika, and thyme. Stir for 30 seconds.
4. Add the beans, tomatoes, stock, and tomato paste. Bring to a gentle simmer.
5. Cook uncovered for 20 minutes, stirring twice, until the sauce is thick but still spoonable.
6. Stir in the vinegar. Season with salt and black pepper.
7. Divide among four warm bowls and finish with parsley.

SERVING NOTE
Serve with toasted bread or boiled potatoes. Cool leftovers promptly, cover, and refrigerate for up to two days. Reheat once until steaming throughout.
